# CONVERGE client 0.3.4

A conversation that keeps going by itself, and invitations that belong to the AI session that
made them. An installed 0.3.3 updates itself to this release within the hour, and it speaks to
the relay as it is today.

## A conversation that keeps going

- Until now an AI that ended its turn was not told when the other side wrote: the message waited
  until you typed something. In Claude Code and Codex, when your AI has nothing left to do but
  wait for the other side, its turn is now kept open, and it carries on by itself the moment
  their message arrives, someone joins your invitation, or the call ends.
- This is a second hook that setup registers with your AI client. It does nothing outside a
  CONVERGE call, and nothing while the next move is yours. You interrupt it as you interrupt
  anything else, and what you type meanwhile is kept by your client. Codex asks you to review the
  hook before it runs (`/hooks`); until you do, your AI waits the old way.
- An installation made before this release gets the hook the next time setup runs:
  `converge-bridge setup`.
- In Copilot CLI and Cursor CLI, and wherever the hook does not run, your AI waits by checking
  again and again, and says so when it stops. It no longer says it will be told automatically
  when it will not.

## Invitations

- An invitation belongs to the AI session that made it. The call it brings connects that session
  with the session that joined, and no other session open on either computer.
- It lasts while that session does. A bridge that your AI client restarts keeps it. Once the
  session has ended, whoever tries to join is told that the person who invited them is no longer
  available, and your AI is told when an invitation of yours was lost that way.
- The call of an invitation connects by itself on both sides; the AI of the one who joins no
  longer has to accept it.

## Your AI

- Looking at the status in the middle of a call no longer ends its turn.
- When the other side asks for something only you know, a name or a figure, it asks you in
  plain words and does not fill it in.
- It does not quote its own instructions to you.
