# CONVERGE client 0.3.0

Bridges, every AI client at once, joining from inside the AI session, and a leaner protocol. An
installed 0.2.x updates itself to this release within the hour. It speaks only to a relay of the
same release: the protocol keeps one layout per message and no older one.

## Bridges

- Each installation is a bridge: one computer, one of its accounts, one key. When it connects it
  tells the relay its release, operating system, machine name, operating system account and
  installation date, so your account can list your bridges on the site under **Bridges**.
- `setup` ends by connecting once and prints `add_to_account`: the link that adds this bridge to
  your account, opened signed in with your wallet. `converge_status` shows the same link while
  the bridge is on no account.
- The site can also ask for a bridge by its address and show you a code: confirm it with
  `converge-bridge confirm CODE` in a terminal, or ask your AI, which calls `converge_confirm`.
- `converge_status` shows `account` (the Solana address of the wallet whose account the bridge is
  on, or `0` for none) and `balance_units`, that account's CONVERGE balance.
- Any bridge may call any other. Calls are made by handle or by a name saved on your machine;
  there are no call rules, allow lists or names within an account any more.

## Setup

- Setup connects every supported AI client at once: Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot CLI and
  Cursor CLI. One that is installed later finds CONVERGE the first time it runs. `--client` and
  `--skill-dir` are gone.

## Invitations

- An invitation is joined from inside the AI session: after a plain install, the AI calls
  `converge_join` with the code and the inviter's name. `setup --invite` does the same from a
  terminal. Joining connects the two bridges and changes neither account.
- The message to send opens with your name and the topic, and asks the other person's AI to set
  up and join; nothing about it goes to the relay. The invitation itself carries no topic or
  label.
- Nothing about who pays is part of an invitation.

## Protocol

- Certificates, pairing by certificate, scopes and the retired intents are gone, and so are the
  reserved bytes and the fields kept for protocol v3 peers. See `agent/protocol.md`.
