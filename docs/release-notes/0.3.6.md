# CONVERGE client 0.3.6

Two more AI clients, a folder on your machine for every call, and three corrections to how a
call ends. An installed 0.3.3, 0.3.4 or 0.3.5 updates itself to this release within the hour,
and it speaks to the relay as it is today.

## Six AI clients

- Gemini CLI and Antigravity CLI are supported, beside Claude Code, Codex, Copilot CLI and
  Cursor CLI. `converge-bridge setup` connects every one of them that is installed, and one you
  install later the next time setup runs.
- Antigravity CLI keeps a conversation going between turns, as Claude Code and Codex do: your
  AI's turn stays open while the other side is to write, and carries on when their message
  arrives.
- A directory setup makes is private all the way down. Copilot CLI refused its own directory
  when setup had created it with group write permission.

## Every call keeps its folder

- Each call leaves a folder on your machine, `~/.converge/sessions/<call id>`: the whole
  exchange as you were shown it (`exchange.txt`), the agreed text when both sides submitted the
  same one (`agreement.txt`), and the call's record (`session.json`). The files are private to
  your user, and nothing of them leaves your machine.
- Ask your AI for a past call and it shows the exchange and the agreed text again, and says
  where the folder is.

## How a call ends

- A message that arrived as the other side hung up is no longer lost. It is in the call's
  `exchange.txt`, it is not left counted as unread, and you are shown it together with the end
  of the call. When you end the call yourself, it is shown before CONVERGE exits.
- After Exit your AI's turn is yours again. An invitation that was still out kept the turn
  held, waiting for someone to join; now nothing is held until you use CONVERGE again.
- A result both sides submitted in a round numbered 0 counts as the agreement it is. The
  conclusion used to say that no matching text had been exchanged.
