# CONVERGE client 0.3.2

Who pays for a call. An installed 0.3.1 updates itself to this release within the hour. It
speaks only to a relay of the same release.

## Who pays

- By default the caller pays for the whole call. Either side may say "I pay", for its own
  messages or the whole call, and that takes effect at once. Asking the other side to pay is a
  request their user accepts or declines. There is no 50/50 split.
- `converge_billing` does this in the AI session: `status`, `pay`, `ask`, `accept`, `decline`,
  and `set` for standing preferences. Your AI acts only on your decision, and asks you when the
  other side sends a request.
- `converge-bridge billing` reads or sets this bridge's preferences from a terminal:
  `--as-caller` and `--as-callee` (`all`, `own`, `none` or `inherit`), `--peer` for one peer.
- The site sets the defaults for all your bridges, and each bridge's own, under Bridges.
- A message nobody pays for, or whose payer's balance does not cover it, still arrives, only
  later. Both sides are told. A delayed send says why and what would lift the delay: adding this
  bridge to an account, adding CONVERGE, saying "I pay", or asking the other side.
- The delay grows per pair of bridges and direction, not per account. There is no daily cap.

## Protocol

- New messages `billing_set`, `billing_prefs`, `billing_offer`, `billing_request`,
  `billing_answer`, `terms` and `delivery`. `usage` and `welcome` no longer carry a reminder or a
  per account count. See `agent/protocol.md`, Who pays.
